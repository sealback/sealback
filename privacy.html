Privacy Policy

Summary

Sealback encrypts and decrypts entirely in your browser. The information you enter, your key files and passphrases are never transmitted to us or anyone else. This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising, does not store anything in your browser and does not load any content from third parties. The only personal data processed is the technical data that is necessary to deliver the website to your browser (see Hosting) and data you send us yourself when you contact us.

Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Libre Solar Technologies GmbH. The full address and contact details can be found in the imprint.

Using Sealback

Keys are created, and information is encrypted and decrypted, by the program code running in your browser. The website has no server-side component that could receive your data, and its Content Security Policy prevents the page from connecting to or loading anything from other servers. This applies to the confidential information you enter, to key files, passphrases and public keys, and to encrypted messages.

Nothing is stored in your browser: no cookies, no local storage, no database. All data is discarded when you close or reload the page, and the decryption page discards it after 10 minutes of inactivity. Key files and encrypted messages are only saved when you download them yourself. To choose the language, the page reads the preferred languages set in your browser. This information is not stored or transmitted.

The public key is contained in the part of the link after the “#”, which browsers do not send to the web server. Links and encrypted messages are passed on by you, for example by email. If you use the button to open your email program, the browser hands the message over to that program. The respective email provider is responsible for how the email is processed; the content remains encrypted.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit this website, GitHub processes technical data that your browser transmits automatically in order to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure. This includes in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page and information about your browser and operating system. This data is not combined with other data sources by us, and we do not have access to it in a form that identifies you.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the reliable and secure delivery of this website.

GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information can be found in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, phone number and the content of your message) in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR otherwise. Our legitimate interest is answering your request. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, for example to GitHub. Data is only transmitted to these websites once you click a link. From then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

Your rights

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)

  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
