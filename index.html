Do you need a password, access data or other confidential information from someone? Sealback creates a key and a link for that person.

Request information Create a key and a link for the person who has the information. Decrypt information Received encrypted information? Open your key file and decrypt it.

How it works

  1. Create a key. The key file is saved on your computer, and you get a link.
  2. Send the link to the person who has the information. They encrypt it and email it back to you.
  3. Open Sealback again, choose “Decrypt information” and open your key file.

Why Sealback?

  • Nothing leaves your browser Encryption and decryption happen on your device.
  • No account, no installation Works in any modern browser.
  • Quantum Safe Hybrid encryption with ML-KEM-768 and X25519.
  • Open standard Messages use the age encryption format.
  • Open source The complete code is public and can be reviewed by anyone.
  • Free and without tracking No costs, no ads, no analytics, no cookies.

How does this work?

Sealback uses age, an open and widely used encryption format.

Quantum Safe keys combine ML-KEM-768 with X25519. Elliptic curve keys use X25519 alone.

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
